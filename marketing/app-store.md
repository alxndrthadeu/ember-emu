## English (U.S.)

**Subtitle (30):** Classic RPGs in your pocket

**Promotional text (170):** Bring your RPG Maker XP, VX and VX Ace games to iPhone. Import them your way, play with touch or a controller, and save whenever you like.

**Description (4000):**

Ember plays classic role-playing games made with RPG Maker XP, VX and VX Ace on your iPhone. Import a game, tap it, and play. No setup.

BRING YOUR GAMES
• Import .zip, .rar, .7z, self-extracting .exe or the game folder, even when the game sits inside subfolders.
• From the Files app and iCloud, from a computer's browser over Wi-Fi, by cable (Mac, Windows or Linux), from the clipboard, or by drag and drop.
• Many games rely on the RPG Maker RTP: import it once and every game of that version uses it.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Handheld-style on-screen controls in portrait and landscape.
• Bluetooth and USB controllers (Xbox, PlayStation, Switch Pro, MFi) and hardware keyboards, with button profiles per game.
• Custom skins, and you can make or import your own.
• Up to 5x fast forward.
• A keyboard for naming your hero, even in older games.

NEVER LOSE PROGRESS
• Quick save at any quiet moment on the map.
• Auto save when you leave the app and every few minutes. Continue picks up right where you stopped, even if iOS closed the app.
• The game's own saves keep their original format, so you can export them or bring saves from elsewhere.

MADE WITH CARE
• MIDI music, game sound on or off, or play along with your own music.
• A shelf with covers, play history and per-game logs.
• A performance overlay with FPS, CPU and memory.
• English, Portuguese and Spanish.
• Includes The Last Ember, a tiny original game to try right away.

Ember does not include commercial games or the RPG Maker RTP. Play games you own or games their authors share for free.

Ember is free software (GPL-3.0) and contains a modified version of mkxp-z-apple-mobile. Source code and licenses: github.com/alxndrthadeu/ember-emu. RPG Maker is a trademark of its respective owners; Ember is not affiliated with them.

## Notas para o revisor (App Review Information › Notes)

Ember is a player for games made with RPG Maker XP, VX and VX Ace (it runs the game's Ruby scripts with the open-source mkxp-z engine, under guideline 4.7). It ships with no commercial games and no RPG Maker runtime files; users import games they own.

To test right away, the app includes a small original game we made, "A Última Brasa" (The Last Ember):
1. Open the Import tab and tap "Sample game" under Other ways (or, on an empty library, "Try the sample game").
2. In the Library, tap the game. On the title screen, press A (or Start) on "New game".
3. Walk with the D-pad. Stand next to the cat by the campfire and press A to talk. Press B to open the game's menu.
4. Tap the gear button to open Ember's menu: speed, quick save (Save State), states, screenshot, sound, quit.

Other features: "Send over Wi-Fi" on the Import tab starts a local web server so a computer on the same network can upload games from a browser (no internet service involved). Ember opens one game per launch: to switch games, close the app from the app switcher; the app explains this when needed.

No account or sign-in is needed.
